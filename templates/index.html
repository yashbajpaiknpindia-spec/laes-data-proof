EXECUTION LOGIC

How paper entries trigger

01 · Leader already underwater

Observed unrealized PnL below zero or current mark adverse to entry.

02 · Better price than leader entry

Same-side simulation enters at current Binance mark after the leader has absorbed an adverse move.

03 · Micro confirmation

Latest 5-minute candle moves in the leader's direction. No confirmation means watchlist only.

04 · Independent risk controls

Paper stop, target, fees, slippage, margin and capital controls are applied separately from the leader.

A paper trade is not a recommendation or guarantee. Recovery probabilities are hidden until a sufficient matched sample exists.
